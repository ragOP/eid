The Insane Deal: every member of The Lions' Den jury backs this product!

A report by Ella Schmitz | Thursday, March 19, 2026

( - It was the most popular episode of the new season of “The Lions’ Den” – the three sisters Anna, Lena and Janina Martin won over the entire jury with their unique product.)

Never before has the entire “Den” jury independently decided to invest millions of euros in a single product.
 After the jury bought an incredible 25% stake in the three sisters’ company, they personally helped the three sisters rebrand and repackage their wonder product.
The three sisters’ products are excellent, but they had problems with marketing. The three sisters, who became famous overnight, have degrees in nutrition science and sports medicine. The investors immediately recognized that this was a great product and that the three sisters “only” needed help with marketing.

The three sisters praised their product as “the biggest step in the history of weight loss”. The investors were initially skeptical, but the three sisters were able to prove the effectiveness of their product with scientific studies. The “wonder capsules” are also popular on social media platforms Instagram and TikTok. There are already thousands of videos about the product. “We were shocked. We had really only expected to get a few tips on marketing. We weren’t even sure whether we could win over a single investor,” Janina said in the episode. After a total of 5 offers from the individual investors, the three sisters were visibly shocked.
“It didn’t feel real. Sitting in front of us were Germany’s most successful entrepreneurs. And they absolutely wanted to be part of our project. I think we underestimated our product the whole time. It was incredible and very emotional for us!” explains Anna. The three sisters are the first contestants in the show’s long history to receive standing ovations and investments from every jury member. Their lives changed overnight. Since this incredible episode was recorded, the three sisters have been working hard and using their investors’ tips.
Clinical trials of the MedicGLP capsules have proven that test subjects using this product were able to drastically reduce their body fat and that this effect continues with ongoing use.

Clinical studies with a total of 1,800 participants proving the product’s effectiveness ultimately convinced the lions. In a recent clinical study with over 500 participants, those who regularly took MedicGLP lost an average of 7.5 kilograms more weight per month than participants in the control group.

Studies on MedicGLP have also sparked great interest in Denmark. The results were similarly impressive, as many of the 1,300 participants recorded significant weight loss clearly attributable to taking MedicGLP. The Danish studies showed an average weight reduction of about 8.3 kilograms per month for those who took MedicGLP regularly. Despite the good experiences and strong sales of the product, the lions were still skeptical of the three sisters. But after the three sisters presented the clinical studies, even the last lion was convinced.

This is how the three sisters convinced the investors

The three sisters put all their energy into developing a formula. The clinical studies convinced every investor with their effectiveness.

Unlike many other products already presented on the show,  MedicGLP has gone through several test series in which the following points were established:

  • MedicGLP consists of special capsules that release the active ingredients into the body with a time delay. Taking one capsule can put the body into a 24-hour fat-burning mode
  • The active ingredient in MedicGLP is immediately absorbed by the stomach lining thanks to the special formula
  • the preparation regulates the body’s metabolism to a normal level
  • regulates blood sugar to an optimal level, making it harder for the body to store fat in cells
  • prevents cravings, so calorie intake is reduced
